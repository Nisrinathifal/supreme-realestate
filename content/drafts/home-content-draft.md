## 10. Deliberately not carried over from the reference

- Price chips, listing cards, "1M properties", cashback percentages: PRD §6.2 (prices, portfolio size, figures).
- Map with pins: PRD §6.2 (locations).
- Feature cards with "Explore listings" / "Learn more": no listings, no product.
- Carousel with pager and benefit copy: no benefits to claim without figures; DESIGN §5 avoids auto-rotating carousels.
- Marquee of the brand name: DESIGN §5 restraint; wordmark appears once, in the footer.
- Smiling person with a phone: no people recognisable without release; no stock.
- Tilted cards and floating motion: REFERENCE §9 (tilt 0°, no float).
